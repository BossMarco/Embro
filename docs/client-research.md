## Website direction

The initial site should be a bilingual, mobile-first lead-generation site for Rio Grande Valley customers. It should make calling, messaging, finding the shop, and requesting a quote immediate.

Recommended core pages:

1. Home — clear custom-apparel value proposition, primary call-to-action, service overview, local trust cues.
2. Services — embroidery, uniforms, shirts, caps, and any additional services confirmed with the client.
3. Gallery — real finished-work photography, organized by product or customer type.
4. Request a quote — garment type, quantity, sizes, logo upload, due date, and contact details.
5. Contact — phone, map, hours, Facebook, and bilingual contact language.

## Content and discovery priorities

- Use the neighborhood and RGV terms naturally in titles and copy: McAllen, Rio Grande Valley, custom embroidery, uniforms, shirts, and caps.
- Add a Google Business Profile link, a location map, correct business hours, and a consistent name/address/phone across the site and social profiles after the client confirms them.
- Favor Spanish/English copy and real work examples over generic stock imagery.
- Collect customer types served, turnaround time, minimum order policy, brands/garments available, pricing approach, and preferred quote/contact workflow before publishing service claims.
